Agents before you have created this skill to reduce known failures and limitations.

## 1. Prepare camera
Read the screen. A mode list that slides in on its own at launch closes when tapped and does not change the mode. Let it close or dismiss it, then read again.

## 2. Choose the mode
Swipe right across the middle of the preview, from about one fifth to four fifths of the screen width, to open the mode list. A swipe from the very edge is Android's Back gesture and leaves the camera. Tap "Switch to Camera Mode" for a photo or "Switch to Video Camera" for a video.

## 3. Capture
Photo: press Shutter once per photo the task asks for, then read the screen. Never press it again to check whether it worked.
Video: press Shutter once to start recording. A recording timer ("00:00") appears. Wait a few seconds, then press Shutter once to stop. If no timer appears, you are still in photo mode and just took a photo: go back to step 2.

## 4. Verify
Open the saved file in a gallery or Files app and read its name and time. A video is saved as VID_….mp4 in Movies. A gallery tile alone is not proof. If no new file exists, say so and start again from step 1.

## 5. Fix
To remove an extra capture, delete that file, then reopen its folder and confirm it is gone. A "Deleted" message is not proof.
